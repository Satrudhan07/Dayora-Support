# Dayora Support

Need help or want to report a problem? Email **[your contact email]** and include your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version. Replies usually arrive within two working days.

## Common questions

**My tasks do not appear on my other device.**
Check that you are signed in to the same Apple ID, that iCloud Drive is on, and that "Sync with iCloud" is on in Dayora → Settings. Close and reopen Dayora after changing it.

**I do not get reminder notifications.**
Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Dayora and allow notifications. In Dayora → Settings, make sure "Remind me at the scheduled time" is on.

**How do I add a task with Siri?**
Say "Hey Siri, add a task in Dayora" or "Hey Siri, add a reminder in Dayora". You can also find Dayora's shortcuts in the Shortcuts app.

**How do I add the widget?**
Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap +, and choose Dayora.

**How do I export or delete my data?**
Dayora → Settings → Your data. Export as JSON or CSV, delete completed tasks, or delete everything. If iCloud sync is on, deleting everything also removes your tasks from iCloud and your other devices.
