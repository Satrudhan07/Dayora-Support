# Dayora Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

Dayora is a task planner and reminder app for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information.

## The short version
- Dayora has **no account** and **no servers**. We do not collect, see, sell or share your tasks or any personal data.
- There is **no advertising, no analytics and no tracking**.

## What the app stores
Your tasks, reminders, lists and settings are stored **on your device**.

## iCloud sync (optional)
If you are signed in to iCloud and leave "Sync with iCloud" on in Settings, Dayora keeps your tasks in **your own private iCloud storage** (Apple CloudKit) so they appear on your other Apple devices. This data belongs to your Apple ID. It is handled by Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and we cannot access it. You can turn sync off in Settings. "Delete all my data" removes your tasks from your device and from iCloud.

## Microphone and speech recognition (optional)
If you choose to speak a task, Dayora uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition to turn your voice into text. Depending on your device and settings, Apple may process the audio on your device or on Apple's servers, as described in Apple's privacy policy. Dayora does not keep recordings.

## Notifications
Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Dayora does not send your information anywhere to do this.

## Siri, Shortcuts and widgets
Siri requests and widgets run on your device and use the same stored tasks. Dayora shares nothing beyond that.

## Exporting and deleting your data
You can export your tasks (JSON or CSV) and delete them at any time from Settings. Deleting the app removes the data on that device; tasks in iCloud can be removed with "Delete all my data" first, or from iPhone Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

## Children
Dayora does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
